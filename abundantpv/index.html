The idea behind the book

Life is a game. And you were never meant to watch it from the bench.

Somewhere between the deadlines, the bills, the noise and the ambition, most of us quietly stepped off the field. We didn't reject God — we just stopped showing up to play the position He gave us. We became spectators of our own faith.

The Lost Player is a short, honest, deeply practical guide that does one thing: it puts you back in the game. No shame. No religious performance. Just a clear, step-by-step path from spiritual drift to a living, daily walk with God — starting exactly where you are today.
